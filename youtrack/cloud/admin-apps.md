# Apps

Apps give you the power to extend YouTrack with additional functionality tailored to your specific needs. You can integrate with other tools, add widgets to display important data, and share workflows. Apps offer a flexible way to expand your YouTrack experience.

You can browse the [JetBrains Marketplace for YouTrack apps](manage-apps-admin.html#install-app-from-repository) to install directly to your YouTrack site. You can also [upload your own custom-built apps](manage-apps-admin.html#upload-apps).

Apps can be used in a variety of locations in YouTrack. You can build widgets that can be added to dashboards or displayed in issues, tickets, or articles. Widgets can also be used to embed dynamic components in areas of the application used to display content.
