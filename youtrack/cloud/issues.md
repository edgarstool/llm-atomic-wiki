# Issues

Issues represent the work your team manages in YouTrack. An issue can mean different things to different teams. For your team, an issue might represent a bug, task, or defect. For another team, it might represent a product feature or the work required to build it.

No matter what an issue represents, YouTrack helps you manage it from the moment it is reported until it is resolved. Use the topics in this section to find and review issues, report new work, update issue details, and collaborate with your team.

## Find and Review Issues

* [Issue Search](search-for-issues.html) — Locate issues that meet specific criteria.

* [Issue List](issues-list.html) — Review and prioritize a list of issues in YouTrack.

## Report and Update Issues

* [Report an Issue](reporting-an-issue.html) — Create an issue to track a bug, task, feature, or other work.

* [Create and Update Issues](create-and-edit-issues.html) — Create issues and update their fields and descriptions.

* [Update Issues with Commands](commands.html) — Update multiple issues in a single action with commands.

## Collaborate on Issues

* [Add an Attachment](add-an-attachment.html) — Upload files and images that provide supporting information.

* [Add Comments](add-a-comment.html) — Discuss an issue and share updates with other users.
